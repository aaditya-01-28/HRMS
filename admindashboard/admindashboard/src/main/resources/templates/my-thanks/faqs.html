The "My Thanks" application is a peer-to-peer recognition platform that allows employees to appreciate each other. By sending a "Thanks", you reward your colleagues with points for their hard work and support. These points can later be redeemed for exciting rewards in the Redemption Store.

To send thanks, navigate to the "Send Thanks" section using the left sidebar. Select your colleague from the dropdown list, enter the number of points you wish to give, and add a personalized message. Your points will be deducted from your wallet and credited to their account instantly!

The Redemption Store is our internal marketplace where you can exchange the points you have earned for rewards such as company merchandise, gift cards, and digital vouchers. Simply browse the store, click "Redeem Now" on your desired item, or add multiple items to your Cart to checkout at once.

You can view your current points balance on the Dashboard or in the top right corner of any page. To see a detailed log of your points earned (credits) and spent (debits), visit the "Transaction History" page. You can easily search or filter past transactions by date.

Physical merchandise will be shipped to the Delivery Address specified in your Profile. Before redeeming merchandise, please ensure your delivery address and pincode are up to date in the "View Profile" section to avoid any delivery delays. Gift cards and vouchers will be sent directly to your registered email.

How do I continue a course from where I left off?

To continue a course, navigate to "My Learnings" from the sidebar and check the "Ongoing" tab. Click the "Continue" button next to your course.

How can I download my course certificate?

Certificates can be downloaded from the "Certificates" tab under the "My Learnings" section once a course is 100% completed.

Why is my course not showing in My Learning?

It may take a few minutes for new enrollments to sync. If it still doesn't appear, try logging out and back in.
